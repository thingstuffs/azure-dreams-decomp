Before delivery, run `python3 tools/lanes/package_preflight.py --root "$REPO" --package "$PACKAGE/apply.py" --log "$TMPDIR/package-preflight.jsonl"` (use `--arg` for each package argument). The tool checks the landing contract, integrity inventories and guards against HEAD, replays every package in `work/native_lane/_pending_packages.json` before yours, then runs levels, graph, status and scrub checks in a disposable worktree. A pass grants no review or byte proof. Rebase and rerun after HEAD or the pending queue changes; then append your repo-relative entry, arguments and `"live_flag":true` to that queue's version-1 `packages` list without replacing other lanes' entries, and include the HEAD/queue hashes and log in your delivery. Keep review fields pending. The orchestrator performs the full landing proofs.
