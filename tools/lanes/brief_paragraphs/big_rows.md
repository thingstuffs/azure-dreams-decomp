## BIG ROWS (read before the rows)

These are whole functions with many pins. Yesterday's astra pack on five such rows took one from 51 pins to 24 (staged copies replaced by aggregate copies from typed source symbols), one 42 -> 39 (a pointer initialisation moved across a branch plus width corrections) and one 41 -> 40 (a doubling written as a shift): see work/native_lane/r62_astra_big/REPORT.md. Work the duck's GROUPS, not single pins: each group of same-class pins usually has ONE cause (a scalarized copy, a page constant that should be a symbol - tools/xform/t86_symaddr.py and t78_aggcopy.py docstrings, a value whose lifetime should be merged with its neighbour - t87_lifetimemerge.py, a parameter or local of the wrong width - t84/t36/t37). A partial result is a success: land every group you can make byte-exact and keep the other pins in place. "Impossible in C" has been wrong every time in this project.
