Cleanliness levels (bytes at or above): **L0 97.4 %, L1 71.2 %, L2 67.6 %** (L2 = no
boilerplate, no dead pins, no raw offset access; see PLAN.md §3). Before today every row was at L0.

What T4 does, concretely: `M2C_FIELD(arg0, s16 *, 8)` and the local `FIELD(p, type, off)` macro
(1,294 files, ~50 k sites — three quarters of all raw offset access, missed by the previous
campaign's census) become `arg0->unk_08` on a per-function `S_<addr>_<n>` struct with explicit
`pad_XX` gaps; same-width conflicts become union views (`.s/.u`, `.v/.n`), overlapping accesses
a union of views, bytes before the pointer a `_pre` record addressed as `base[-1]`; `void *`
parameters and locals are retyped. When the struct form changes codegen (aliasing changes the
scheduler's freedom), a delta-debugging search keeps the minimal set of sites as explicit
casts: 449 rows needed that, 954 sites.

## Pin census (single-site erasure, full: 22,505 sites in 2,066 rows)

47 % of pin sites are dead on their own; 4 % of pinned rows (92) become pin-free that way.
Load-bearing pins by breakage class: broad 18 %, reorder-only 18 %, reg-rename 13 %,
length-drift 9 %, code-motion 8 %, block-order 6 %, addressing 6 %, slot-rotation 5 %,
delay-slot 4 %, const-remat 4 %. Context: `ASM_KEEP` before `return` is dead ~70 % of the
time; `ASM_TAILSLOT_PIN` before a call never. Details and the alternative per class:
`docs/PIN_CENSUS.md`, `docs/PLAN.md` §9. Reorder-only and recolouring pins encode instruction
schedules that source order cannot always express; their honest product form is the pin plus a
measured reason, which T6 writes as `/* MATCH pin: ... */` from the T2 verdicts (1,983 rows).
